The former `apps/web` root redirect now serves as Love Reminder's Vietnamese public acquisition landing page. Its acquisition approach is intent-first: a clear no-account CTA plus three internal gift-guide entry pages (`/qua-sinh-nhat`, `/qua-ky-niem`, `/qua-cho-nguoi-yeu`) and a public sitemap/robots policy. The review console remains protected at `/review`, `/sources`, and `/runs`.

**Why:** The public website needs indexable pages that answer gift-intent searches, while the unpublished merchant review console must remain private.

**How to apply:** Keep public content separate from console routes; never put merchant-review data or console credentials in public pages. Replace generic guide copy with curated, verified Vietnamese merchant recommendations only after the catalog is available.
