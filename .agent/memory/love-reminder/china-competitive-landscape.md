China has direct adjacent products: **小纪念** is an individual anniversary assistant with gift-preparation reminders and lunar-calendar support; **恋爱记** is a broader shared couple space (anniversaries, shared schedule, couple alarm, lists, health tracker, games/community); newer **念念不忘** positions AI greeting-card creation and gift recommendations beside relationship/family date reminders.

**Product implication:** Love Reminder should not frame the category as novel. Its differentiation can be Vietnamese-first language/culture, support for partners *and* family/friends, proactive meaningful-action guidance, and trustworthy locally approved merchant recommendations rather than generic couple-space features.
